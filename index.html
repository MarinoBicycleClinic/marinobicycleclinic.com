About

Marino Bicycle Clinic was established with one mission: to bring a reliable, high quality Bicycle Repair, electric bikes, mopeds, scooters. My passion for excellence is what inspired me to open up my shop, and continues to drive me to this day. All my repairs are completed with meticulous detail, resulting in a product that is as good as new.

Your satisfaction is my priority

Do you have something that needs fixing? Marino Bicycle Clinic is here for all your repair needs. When possible, I use original parts for repairs, so anything you bring to me will be returned to you in mint condition. Feel free to call or text me with any questions you may have. I'm invested in customer service and always happy to help.
